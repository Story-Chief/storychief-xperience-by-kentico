# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus feedback on ideas and technical outcomes.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal, customer, or confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately inflammatory conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. Report unacceptable behavior privately to [support@storychief.io](mailto:support@storychief.io). Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when warranted.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
